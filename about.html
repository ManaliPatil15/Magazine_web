About Mahodadhi

A student magazine in three languages, made by a team that loves words, pictures and good design.

A great ocean, one drop at a time

Mahodadhi (महोदधि) is a Sanskrit word for the great ocean. Our magazine takes its name from that idea: every student voice is a drop, and together they make something vast.

Each issue is written, illustrated, designed and photographed by students, in Marathi, Hindi and English.

Meet Team Abhiyanta

अभियंता means engineer: someone who builds. Team Abhiyanta builds pages instead of bridges, and turns scattered ideas into a magazine you can hold.

How an issue is made

From a rough idea to a printed page, every issue follows the same five steps.

1

Idea

The team picks a theme and plans the sections for the issue.

2

Call for entries

Students send poems, stories, sketches and photographs.

3

Review and edit

Section heads read every piece and work with writers on edits.

4

Design and layout

Designers and illustrators give each page its shape and colour.

5

Launch

The issue goes online and into print for the whole campus.
